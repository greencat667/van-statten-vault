## 1. Museum opening, beat by beat (transcript, paraphrased; quotes kept short)

1. **TARDIS lands in the dark.** Stage direction: the TARDIS materialises in a dimly lit area with carpet and display cases. [screen] T
   - Floor is **carpeted** per the transcriber. [screen, transcriber's description] T
2. **Where and when.** The Doctor says they are on Earth, in Utah, about half a mile underground, in 2012. Rose works out she would be 26. [screen] T
3. **He looks at a display case**, then **finds a light switch**; the room becomes clear. Rose calls it a great big museum. [screen] T
   - Lights come up from a switch: room is dark first, then lit. Good for a two-state lighting setup in the model. [screen] T
4. **Exhibits named by the Doctor** (in this order): chunks of meteorite; Moon dust; the milometer from the Roswell spaceship. He remarks someone must have spent a fortune. [screen] T
5. **Rose spots a stuffed Slitheen arm** ("a Slitheen's arm. It's been stuffed."). [screen] T
6. **The Cyberman head.** The Doctor finds it and greets it; he calls it an old friend, then corrects himself to enemy, and says the stuff of nightmares has been reduced to an exhibit. [screen] T
7. **Signal.** Rose asks if the signal comes from the head; the Doctor says it is "stone dead"; the signal is alive and calling for help. [screen] T
8. **Alarm.** The Doctor touches the display case (TARDIS wiki: the glass casing of the Cyberman exhibit) and an alarm sounds. [screen] T, TW
9. **Guards.** Armed guards rush in "from all sides" and cut the pair off from the TARDIS. So the room has several entrances and the TARDIS stands in open floor space between them. [screen] T
10. Rose's closing joke: the Doctor would be Exhibit A. [screen] T

Return to the museum at the end: the Doctor, Rose and Adam come back to the TARDIS in the museum; Goddard plans to fill the base with cement. The Doctor's line there ("a little piece of home") follows the Dalek's death; Adam slips into the TARDIS. [screen] T, TWE

## 4. Physical description of the museum room

Text sources say very little; this is mostly for frame checking.
- Dim at first, then lit from a light switch. [screen] T
- Carpeted floor. [screen, transcriber] T
- Display cases, at least one glass case (Cyberman). [screen] T, TW
- Multiple entrances: guards come "from all sides". [screen] T
- Fictional geography: 53 floors down, the lowest level. Goddard: intruders arrested "fifty three floors down"; Van Statten says the Doctor was caught "right next to the Cage". So the museum and the Cage are on the same lowest level. [screen] T; TWE/TWC (Almanac: Exhibit Room on 53rd floor)
- Real room: a gallery space in the National Museum Cardiff, a 1920s Edwardian Baroque building, dressed by the Series 1 art department (set decorator Liz Griffiths, supervising art director Stephen Nicholas, standby art director Julian Luxton, property master Adrian Anscombe). [prod] SS crew list. Which gallery was used is not given.
- [recall] Cool blue-grey low-key light with pools on lit cases; dark ceiling; the TARDIS stands in a clear aisle between cases. Verify against frames.
- Ceiling, walls, columns, doors: **no text source describes them.** Use frames.

## 5. The Cage

On screen [screen] T:
- Approached through an "Outside the Cage" control area: a desk with monitors where Van Statten and Goddard watch; Simmons controls the power ("I've had to take the power down").
- A **heavy door** that clangs shut and locks behind the Doctor. The lock later has a billion combinations, which the Dalek works through (TW).
- **Dark inside** at first. A white light blinks beside a blue glow (dome light and eyestalk), then lights come up.
- The Dalek is **held in chains**; when restored it breaks them.
- Gauntlets handed to visitors: Simmons says the last man to touch it burst into flames.
- A **lever** in the cell that sends electricity through the Dalek (the Doctor pulls it; Simmons turns it off). TW
- Torture: a technician in a **protective suit** uses a **chainsaw** on it; later Simmons uses a **big drill** on its casing. [screen] T
- Cameras/monitors carry the Cage to the office (big wall TV) and to Adam's workshop. [screen] T
- After escaping, the Dalek smashes a **computer terminal** (manipulator arm) to absorb power; it goes to the **wall monitor** outside the Cage. [screen] T, TW
- A separate cell, "Doctor's cage": the Doctor is stripped to the waist and chained spread-eagled, scanned by a painful laser. [screen] T
- TM claims a stock of liquid oxygen is next to the cell. [fan] TM
- [recall] The Dalek sits on a raised circular platform in the middle of a dark industrial room, chained to anchor points around it, with hard top and side light. Verify against frames.

Concept: Shearman modelled the Doctor/Dalek cell scene on Clarice Starling and Hannibal Lecter. [prod] SS, TW

Filming location: Millennium Stadium interiors, 26 Oct to 3 Nov 2004 (see section 3). [prod/inferred] SS

## 6. The 2005 Dalek prop

- Built by Mike Tucker's BBC Model Unit. The 1985 *Revelation* mould was available, but Russell T Davies wanted the original 1960s shape. Moulds were taken from two fan-built Daleks owned by Andrew Beech. [prod] SS, TW
- Made: **two skirt sections (one pristine, one damaged)** and **two upper sections (one opened, one closed)**. [prod] SS, TW
- Height: chosen partly so it was **roughly as tall as Billie Piper**. [prod] SS. Billie Piper is about 1.63 m (5 ft 4 in) [fan]. Another account: height chosen so the eyestalk is level with the Doctor's eyes [fan] via search summary. A replica builder's finished build is 5.5 ft (1.68 m) [fan] 2SP. Working figure: about 1.63 to 1.68 m to the dome top.
- Operator seated inside (Barnaby Edwards); only the dome was remote controlled. [prod] SS
- **Hemispheres: 56**, fixed in **four rows** on the skirt panels. [fan] 2SP (56 repeats) and a Dalek glossary (56 in four rows; 48 on New Paradigm). So 14 per row (arithmetic). Each is ringed by a black ring; replica plans say the rings are cast from an automotive oil seal. [fan]
- Replica part sizes (not screen-verified): 4 in (about 10 cm) hemispheres; 110 mm gun ball; 4 in arm balls; three neck rings with eight neck supports; shoulder slats in three sizes. [fan] 2SP. Plans: Project Dalek's 2005 plans, built from screen-used props; one builder warns some dimensions are wrong. Dimensions.com gives only a 1.52 to 1.98 m range for all Daleks.
- Shoulder slat count: **not found in text**. Count from frames.
- Colour: **bronze** (Davies' choice from several metallic finishes; gold is also used informally). Visible bolts and rivets on the casing. [prod] SS, WPD
- Dome lights: Davies asked for lights modelled on the 1960s Dalek **films** (Peter Cushing films). [prod] WPD. On screen the dome lights flash white as it speaks, and the eyestalk glows blue. [screen] T. Replica note: blue neon under the skirt and a blue light-up eye [fan].
- Redesign led by Edward Thomas and the BBC Wales art department, concept art by Matt Savage (Bryan Hitch was also concept artist). [prod] WPD, SS
- Abilities shown: plunger arm with suction, middle section swivels for 360-degree fire, force field melts bullets, it levitates up stairs, the casing opens to show the mutant, and the skirt hemispheres detach to form a sphere around it at the end. [screen] T, TW
- **Battle damage in "Dalek":** a damaged skirt was built [prod]. On screen the Doctor sees a cracked, worn casing and a gun that won't fire (TW). Rose's touch leaves a golden handprint; the Dalek regenerates its casing and becomes pristine. [screen] T, TW. [recall] Scorching and scratches, with some hemispheres damaged on the cell-version skirt. Verify against frames.

## 7. The Cyberman head

- Design: the **Revenge of the Cybermen (1975)** model, known by its **corrugated (coiled) handlebar side handles** and the **gun on top of the helmet**. [fan] TW story notes; TM agrees ("thicker, ridged handlebars"). The TARDIS wiki files it as a "CyberNomad". TWCy
- Revenge-era helmet in general: the weapon is built into the helmet and fired by touching it; the side handles are coiled; the Cyber-Leader's helmet is black, the troopers' silver. [fan] TARDIS wiki "Cyber-helmet" / "CyberNomad". So the exhibit should be a **silver trooper head**, not the black Leader. [inferred]
- Continuity oddity: the plaque says it was found in the London sewers in 1977, which points to *The Invasion* (1968), yet the head is the 1975 design. [fan] TM
- Prop provenance (original 1975 helmet, later replica, or exhibition piece): **not found in any text source.** Revenge costumes were newly made in 1975, derived from *The Invasion*. [fan] Wikipedia, Revenge of the Cybermen
- Display: in a **glass case** with a plaque; the Doctor touches the glass and sets off the alarm. [screen/fan] T, TW
- [recall] Standard features of the 1970s head: teardrop eye holes, a rectangular mouth slot, side "ear" discs feeding the handlebars, and the lamp-like gun on the forehead or crown. Verify against frames.
- Later reference: in *The Age of Steel* Rose recognises the Cybermen's "handle shapes" from this museum head. [screen, later story] TWCy
