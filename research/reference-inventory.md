## Dalek (2005 prop)

Bronze with darker hemispheres; four rows of 14 hemispheres (56, fan glossary); a cracked front skirt panel; dome lights angled outwards; blue eye on a disc-ringed stalk; the plunger on the viewer's left and the gun on the right (V6); neck rings over a grille; about 1.65 m tall (Billie Piper's height, P).
